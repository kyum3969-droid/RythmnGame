# 2-1 노래: 김 빅토르의 포스트펑크 (제미나이 프롬프트)

2-1 2페이즈(4라인 분류 슈트)에 깔 곡. 1페이즈의 반장 소울(88)은 지금처럼 게임이 합성하고, **빅토르의 8분 베이스가 혼자 들어오는 데서부터**(게임 112박, 축이 돌기 직전) 이 녹음 곡을 튼다. 그래서 곡은 베이스 혼자로 시작한다.

- **노래 길이와 시간표는 적지 않는다.** 길이나 구간 시각을 적으면 곡이 중간에 끊기는 일이 잦았다. 구간 순서만 적고, 끝은 페이드 없이 분명한 마지막 한 방으로.
- 124 BPM, D단조, 4/4. 받으면 실제 템포와 첫 다운비트, 구간을 재서 4라인 차트를 곡에 맞춘다(1-5 때처럼).
- **가사 없는 연주곡**(목소리 없음). 멜로디는 신스 리드의 훅이 맡는다.

## 프롬프트 (그대로 붙여 넣기)

```
An instrumental post-punk / cold wave track. No vocals, no singing, no spoken words, no voice of any kind. 124 BPM, 4/4, D minor. Driving, cold and melancholic, but with a grin underneath. Ends with one clear final hit, no fade-out.

Mood: a night shift in a huge logistics warehouse. A man in his forties who has worked these lines for fifteen years is sent over from the next line to save a slow one. He talks and jokes all night, but his music is cold and lonely: someone who is at home everywhere on the floor and nowhere outside it. Fluorescent lights, conveyor belts, boxes sliding down into four chutes. Think of 1980s Soviet new wave and today's Eastern European post-punk revival, with a steady machine pulse.

Instruments: a picked electric bass playing driving straight eighth notes on the root, up front in the mix. A drum machine (LinnDrum or similar): kick on 1 and 3, a big gated snare on 2 and 4, straight eighth hi-hats, an open hat on the "and" of 4, tom fills at the ends of sections. A clean electric guitar drenched in chorus, playing eighth-note arpeggios high on the neck, with a dotted-eighth delay. A cold analog synth lead (square and saw) carrying the melody: a simple, memorable minor hook that comes back every chorus. A dark synth pad in the choruses. No acoustic drums, no brass, no strings, no piano.

Chords: verse Dm | Bb | F | C, chorus Bb | C | Dm | A.

Structure:
Intro: the bass alone, straight eighth notes on D, nothing else. Then the hi-hats creep in, a rising noise sweep and a snare roll.
Drop: drums, guitar and bass hit together on the downbeat with a crash.
Verse 1: bass, drums and chorus guitar. The synth plays one long note every other bar.
Pre-chorus: the snare doubles, the pad swells.
Chorus: the full band, the synth lead plays the hook, kick added on the "and" of 3.
Verse 2: back down, the guitar takes a counter-melody of its own.
Chorus: the hook again.
Bridge: the drums drop to kick and claps, the bass keeps its eighths, the guitar and synth answer each other in short phrases, then a snare roll back in.
Final chorus: everything, the hook doubled an octave up on the synth.
Outro: the bass alone again for a moment, then the whole band on one last D minor hit.
```

## 받으면 할 일
- 실제 템포, 첫 다운비트, 구간 시각을 재서 기록.
- 게임: 112박(베이스 혼자)부터 녹음 곡을 틀고, 120박 드롭을 곡의 드롭에 맞춤. 4라인 차트를 곡의 베이스·스네어·기타·훅에 맞춰 다시 짬.
