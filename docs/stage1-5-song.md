# 1-5 노래: 디스 배틀 — 제미나이(Lyria) 프롬프트

1-5 2페이즈(32–72마디)에 깔 랩 배틀. 다툼 끝에 하은이 지우를 밀쳐내자, 자기 벨트로 돌아온 지우가 "좋아요. 그럼 박으로 해요." 하고 비트를 튼다. 둘이 마디를 주고받으며 서로를 디스한다. 게임 템포 그대로 **104 BPM**, 4/4, E 단조, **1:35**(41마디). 1-6 노래처럼 글로벌 출시용 영어 가사(한국어는 자막).

노래를 받으면 할 일:
- 실제 박과 차례가 바뀌는 지점을 찾아 2페이즈 차트와 `battle` 표를 노래에 맞춘다.
- 지금 있는 임시 비트(`diss` 구간)를 받은 음원으로 바꾼다.
- 실제로 부른 시간대로 영어 가사 + 한국어 자막 파일을 만든다.

## 게임과 맞춤 (1마디 = 2.31초)

| 노래 | 마디(게임) | 차례 | 게임에서 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:09 인트로 | 32–35 | 지우 비트 | 지우가 비트를 틀고 도발("누나 박이 그렇게 잘났으면, 따라와 봐요!"). 34마디부터 지우 상자 |
| 0:09–0:28 지우 벌스 | 36–43 | 지우 | 지우가 **일부러** 하은 벨트로 상자를 던져 떨어뜨림 |
| 0:28–0:46 하은 벌스 | 44–51 | 하은 | 하은 벨트에 정박으로 꽂힘, 지우 벨트는 빔 |
| 0:46–1:05 주고받기 | 52–59 | 2마디씩 지우·하은·지우·하은 | 차례마다 벨트가 바뀜 |
| 1:05–1:14 브레이크다운 | 60–63 | 1마디씩 지우·하은·지우·하은 | 한 마디짜리 긴 화물이 위·아래 번갈아 |
| 1:14–1:32 끝판 | 64–71 | 둘 동시에 | 떨어뜨림과 하은 상자가 번갈아, 끝 두 마디는 박마다 둘이 같이 |
| 1:32–1:35 마지막 한 방 | 72 | 둘 | 두 벨트 같은 박 하나, 그리고 정적 |

비트도 차례를 따라 바뀐다. 지우 차례는 밝은 퓨처펑크 붐뱁(1페이즈 지우 곡의 결), 하은 차례는 1-4 하은 붐뱁을 거칠고 어둡게. 하은 차례로 넘어갈 때 레코드 스크래치, 지우 차례로 넘어갈 때 보컬 찹 → 게임의 조명(하은 빨강, 지우 하늘)과 박마다 바닥 진동이 같은 자리에서 바뀐다.

가사 원칙: 반장·허리 얘기 없음. **꿈은 직접 말하지 않는다**(하은은 "Rent. And more." 정도로만, 사정은 1-6에서). 욕 없음.

## 프롬프트 (그대로 붙여 넣기)

```
A tense hip-hop diss battle between two young night-shift warehouse workers, a guy and a girl, trading verses. English rap vocals, no singing. 1:35 long, 104 BPM, 4/4, E minor. Clean lyrics, no profanity.

Characters and voices:
- JIWOO (male, 20): cocky, playful, bouncy, a little whiny, quick flow with ad-libs ("yah!", "uh-huh"). He thinks rules are for robots.
- HAEUN (female, 23): low, cold, clipped, deadpan, hits every word exactly on the beat, menacing restraint, never raises her voice until the very end.

The beat changes with whoever is rapping:
- JIWOO's beat: bright, funky boom bap with a future-funk flavour: slap bass, disco chord stabs on the off-beats, open hi-hats, a four-on-the-floor kick under the boom bap.
- HAEUN's beat: dark, gritty, dusty boom bap: heavy kick, a cracking snare and clap on 2 and 4, a deep 808, a minor Rhodes stab, vinyl crackle.
- Every switch to HAEUN starts with a record scratch. Every switch to JIWOO starts with a chopped vocal stab. The switches land exactly on the bar line.

Structure with timestamps (one bar = 2.3 seconds):
0:00–0:09 Intro (4 bars): JIWOO's beat fades in loud and confident. One ad-lib from JIWOO on the last bar: "Keep up if you can."
0:09–0:28 Verse 1, JIWOO (8 bars): his beat, cocky and bouncy.
0:28–0:46 Verse 2, HAEUN (8 bars): hard switch to her dark beat after a record scratch. Cold and precise.
0:46–1:05 Back and forth (8 bars): two bars each, JIWOO, HAEUN, JIWOO, HAEUN; the beat switches every two bars.
1:05–1:14 Breakdown (4 bars): the drums drop to just bass and claps, half-time feel; one bar each, JIWOO, HAEUN, JIWOO, HAEUN, short lines with space.
1:14–1:32 Final clash (8 bars): both beats slammed together, double-time hi-hats, a crash on every bar, the two of them cutting each other off line by line; the last two bars they rap together.
1:32–1:35 Ending: one huge hit on the downbeat of the last bar, then dead silence. No fade out.

Lyrics:

[Intro – JIWOO, ad-lib]
Keep up if you can.

[Verse 1 – JIWOO]

You catch it, you complain, then you're back to the clock
Straight lines, no swing, every move by the book
Never cracked a smile, never stole a look
I got my own rhythm, I got my own lane
You're stuck on the beat while I'm out here making rain

[Verse 2 – HAEUN]
You throw it, it falls, and it lands on me
Every box you drop is a box I carry
You call it a flow, I call it a mess
You sleep through the shift and I clean up the rest
You talk about tomorrow like it's already yours
I'm counting the minutes to the next set of doors
Keep your rhythm, kid, keep it loud in your ears
Some of us can't stop long enough to hear

[Back and forth]
JIWOO: Noona, why so cold, did the night shift steal your heart?
JIWOO: Every time I start, you just tear it apart
HAEUN: Every time you start, I'm the one who has to finish
HAEUN: You play, I pay. That's the difference.
JIWOO: Then drop the watch for once, let the beat take the lead
JIWOO: You don't know what you want, I know what I need
HAEUN: You don't know what I need, you don't know what I've seen
HAEUN: Stay up on your stage. I'll stay down at the machine.

[Breakdown]
JIWOO: Then say it. What's it for?
HAEUN: Rent. And more.
JIWOO: That's it? That's your life?
HAEUN: That's the beat. Keep time.

[Final clash]
JIWOO: I throw it! / HAEUN: I catch it!
JIWOO: You're stiff! / HAEUN: You're careless!
JIWOO: My beat! / HAEUN: My belt!
JIWOO: My way! / HAEUN: Every day!
JIWOO: Can't keep up? / HAEUN: Watch me.
JIWOO: One more box! / HAEUN: One more drop!
BOTH: Tick-tock on the line, and nobody's gonna stop
BOTH: Tick-tock on the line, and nobody's gonna stop

(one huge hit, then silence)
```
